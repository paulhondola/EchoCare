EchoCare · security and privacy

Health data about people living alone deserves a system built to protect it.

EchoCare calls seniors, reads their watch and home sensor, and hands emergencies to a DAS dispatcher. Here is how we keep what we learn about them safe.

Three promises

Only the right people

  • Nobody can sign up. A medic or DAS adds each person by phone number.
  • Every request is checked on our server: who you are and whether this senior is yours.
  • Family see that she's OK and where she lives. Never what she said.
GDPR Art. 5(1)(f), 25

As little as possible

  • We never record a voice. Speech becomes text at the phone network.
  • Watch pulse readings are deleted after 24 hours.
  • Location is sent only with an alarm. No tracking.
GDPR Art. 5(1)(c)

People decide

  • The AI holds the conversation. Fixed, tested rules set green, yellow or red.
  • Every red goes to a human dispatcher who talks to the senior.
  • The medic confirms anything the AI reads from a medical record.
GDPR Art. 22

What we never keep

Voice recordings Location history Cameras or microphones at home Health details in notifications Secrets on the watch or sensor box

For the technical reader

One door to the dataAll reads and writes go through one FastAPI service that checks grants per senior. Out-of-reach records answer "not found".
Signed telephonyEvery Twilio callback and the live-call socket are verified by HMAC signature; the call link works once.
AI with no handsThe model can't place a call, set a code or pick who is alerted. Alarm phrases are matched before it reads a word.
Checked extractionEvery condition read from a medical record must quote it word for word, verified by the server, then confirmed by the medic.
Consent with proofA versioned text and the deciding key press or words, kept as a history. Withdrawal stops all calls.
Right to erasure & exportOne button exports everything about a senior as JSON (Art. 15, 20); the button beside it removes everything from our database and file store, the medical PDF included, in one transaction (Art. 17). Erasure waits while a call or an emergency is still open.
No easy gotchasAPI docs hidden in production, framing blocked on the dashboard, no app backups that could carry a session to another phone, dependency scanning in CI.
Guard rails in codeDemo shortcuts refuse to run in production; a laptop can't run against a cloud database. Hundreds of automated tests on every commit.

Before a real pilot

The demo uses fictitious people. Real seniors need these steps first, with DAS as data controller.

  1. Data-processing agreements with every processor, and an EU-hosted language model.
  2. A data protection impact assessment and a record of processing activities.
  3. Retention periods for transcripts and events, set by DAS.
  4. A second sign-in factor for staff accounts.

The legal basis for a public social service is DAS's to confirm with its data protection officer. We ask every senior for consent on the first call either way.

A · One page, caregivers saved as they are added (recommended)

Everything is visible at once on the tablet: senior and address on the left, caregivers on the right.

  • „Adaugă” saves the draft first if it is new, then adds the caregiver immediately, so a caregiver in the room can sign in on the spot (UC-01 step 4).
  • „Salvează ciorna” saves the senior and the address. „Trimite la DAS” saves, then submits.
  • A refused caregiver number shows its error next to that one row; nothing else is affected.

B · Four steps

Senior, then address, then caregivers, then a review screen with „Trimite la DAS”.

  • Each „Continuă” saves, so the draft always exists by the caregivers step.
  • Reads well on a phone; on a tablet most of the screen is empty and the medic cannot see the whole registration until step 4.
  • More screens and more navigation state to build and test.

C · One page, one save for everything

Two fixed caregiver slots. The whole form, caregivers included, goes to the server on „Salvează”.

  • One button, the most familiar form behaviour.
  • One press is several requests. If the senior saves and the second caregiver's number is refused, the page must explain a half-saved state.
  • A caregiver in the room cannot sign in until the medic saves.

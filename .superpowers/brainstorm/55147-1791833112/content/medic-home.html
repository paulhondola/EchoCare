A · A grid of cards; everything opens full screen (recommended)

The tablet shows the list as a two-column grid. Tapping a draft opens the four-step form; tapping any other senior opens their page. Both take the whole screen, with a back arrow.

  • One layout rule: more columns on a wide screen. No extra library.
  • The same navigation on tablet and phone, so one set of screens to build and test.
  • The medic cannot see the list and a patient at the same time. That can come with the codes panel of UC-12.

B · List on the left, the patient on the right

The tablet keeps the list visible and shows the selected senior's page, or the four-step form, in the right pane. A phone falls back to A.

  • Uses the tablet's width, and fills the empty space the four-step form leaves.
